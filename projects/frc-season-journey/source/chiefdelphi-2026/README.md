# 4744 — 2026 Open Alliance reference images

These are the actual REBUILT-season images in the 1 March 2026 update, **post 76**, not the 2025 REEFSCAPE offseason photos near the beginning of the same thread.

Source: [Week 7 – Third Time Lucky](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/ninjas-4744-2026-build-thread-open-alliance/505741/76).

- `rear-intake.jpeg`: the rear ground intake, rollers, side plates and deployment racks.
- `assembled-robot.jpeg`: the full robot with FUEL; useful for relative ball size and the separation of rear intake and front shooter.
- `robot-side.jpeg`: low indexer bed, clear walls, rear mechanism and chassis.
- `shooter.jpeg`: curved aluminium cheeks, pulleys and the front shooter assembly.

The user's later workshop images remain the reference for the final net hopper and consolidated broad shooter rollers. The user's latest correction fixes orientation: rear intake deploys outward; balls travel forward through the robot and leave the front shooter. The hopper net may flex; no extra expanding-hopper mechanism is invented.

Dimensions of the robot are illustrative photo-based proportions, not measured CAD. All real FUEL spheres use 15 cm diameter in one centimetre coordinate system; field dimensions come from the official 2026 manual. Images are only local reference material and are excluded from exported media and the sharing ZIP. Exact image links are in `references.json`.

The linked team clips were inspected at stowed and extended positions: [rack deployment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3edhUx44qBg) and [side view of moving intake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFC3JShYqfc). The animated carriage translates along inclined racks, preserving their length and the side-plate geometry.

Reference media stay in the local authoring workspace and are not included in this publication commit or public player.
